<br>
A creature who sees many times,<br>
Practically by having more eyes.<br>
Scammer by day then at night.<br>
A fly on your corpse like many flies.<br>
<br>
A bengali vampire officially,<br>
I prowl usually in the dark.<br>
All the streets at night my haunt,<br>
Prefer to kill discreetly in a park.<br>
<br>
Drawn to the scenic at night,<br>
I can see in the city darkness.<br>
People like the stories of us,<br>
Like women who like to undress.<br>
<br>
There's nothing christian about us,<br>
God is sort of the adult's santa.<br>
You can't show a cross to stop me,<br>
Or by chanting Rama or Harekrishna.<br>
<br>
Ostentatiously open my fly,<br>
I fuck you before you know.<br>
You are a corpse easily,<br>
I relish your sweet meat slow.<br>
<br>
Carefully biting the flesh,<br>
Sucking the blood stale.<br>
Rotten is cool worms full,<br>
My face turns others pale.<br>
<br>
In the day I run it brisk,<br>
Scam is the only thing that sells.<br>
Gullible fools with raging greed,<br>
Easy to lure into my spell.<br>
<br>
Rice pulling or red mercury,<br>
Hydrogen gas facility and more.<br>
Like leaders we morph the tales,<br>
I invent scams not known before.<br>
<br>
Once we get the money from them,<br>
Never pick up afterwards any call.<br>
The cops are with us always,<br>
It's their fault that they fall.<br>
<br>
Then in the night as a blood blight,<br>
The scars never heal.<br>
Not a stereo pin-prick but a gash,<br>
Fun the merits of my steal.<br>
<br>
Livers and heart I keep as art,<br>
Souvenirs of my trade.<br>
Every night I bite another win,<br>
I am what I have made.<br>
