It may be helpful if I, as a US outsider but a former H1B, add my halfwits to this debate. And even though the temperature is tepid—the heat of displeasure having migrated en masse to Trump’s grotesqueries, moral or imagined, depending on your favorite propaganda outlet—it’s still hot on both sides, internally at least, if you take the rectal temperatures of the MAGA crowd and the job-insecure *desi* segment: deep-in-debt Indians in America who can’t vent either where they are or to the media back home in India. The *godi* media, as they’re now derisively called, are busy crooning hosannas to the greatness of the greatest king, Narendra Damodardas Modi, who’s been ruling since 2014. If you think I’m picking a side, don’t. My disaffection is bipartisan and borderless. My only claim here is this: I’ve been on the H1B long enough—two rounds of it—to notice things worth sharing.

Just like the amount of melanin doesn’t entitle you to anything outside the UV index where it evolved, all supremacist claims—white, Indian, Chinese—are hot air. Americans should know better: the entire field of computer science, which fuels the H1B wars (not chemistry, not biology), was born in the U.S. The other sciences? They’re even more foreign-populated. The PhD students in physics and biomedical labs aren’t MAGA disciples; they’re immigrants chasing dreams, stipends, and the faint hope of a green card.

The reason America became the world’s scientific womb isn’t random luck. It’s because, unlike most of the world, the U.S. government funded basic science generously, even when it didn’t produce quarterly profits. That long leash—let scientists dream without leash yanks—resulted in most of the books I studied from as a computer science student. My master’s was in the U.S., and no, I wasn’t gung-ho or brilliant. Just curious. And while the H1B visa was a pressure cooker, it also saved me from India—a country allergic to merit and invested deeply in moral decay.

In India, if you’re a religious charlatan, a politician with no soul, a businessman without ethics, a murderer, or a proficient backstabber, you thrive. If you’re honest, you starve. If you’re principled, you’re trampled. There’s no interest in thinkers, professionals, or builders—just a vast banquet of freeloaders and frauds hiding under layers of Bollywood glitz, mythological fluff, and garam masala ads. Escaping India is the only rational act a rational Indian can do while still in India.

I don’t have a dog in the U.S. immigration fight—I’m not interested in moving back or staying. But Americans need to drop their fantasies and look at their real problem: it’s not about skin tone. It’s about labor economics. Screw up Maslow’s hierarchy enough, and you don’t just lose skilled workers—you lose curiosity. You lose the guy who likes engineering, not because it pays, but because it’s interesting. You lose me. And no, I’m not superior—just engaged. And engagement matters. You don’t build civilizations on fear and obedience. You build them with wonder.

This “emerging from a darker room” moment—when a foreigner first arrives—is something no American can experience. They’re born in the light of opportunity. To them, it’s not special. To us, it’s everything. And yes, coding can be dry. Math even more so. But I stayed because I was curious. I worked for whatever wage—not because I was cheap labor but because I liked the work. I still do.

If we’re debating labor and value, why not start with minimum wage? Why not ask why it hasn’t been raised in forever? That would shake billionaire ears more than any border wall fantasy. But that’s not where the rage is directed.

Remember the Y2K bug? That’s what brought Indian programmers into America in droves. Fast-forward to 2025, and maybe America won’t need foreigners—or humans at all. But the few it *will* need will be curious minds, not obedient ones. It won’t matter what they smell like or how brown their skin is. It’ll matter what they can imagine. Elon—possibly nuts, sure—is still one of those minds. He gets attention for the wrong reasons, but he *thinks forward*. And that’s the point.

But MAGA, manage your expectations. Free speech absolutism? Not *your* speech. That phrase is a marketing lie—like “he’s a jolly good fellow”—sung over corpses with black-and-white TV morals. What are you smoking?

Life is messy. Binary thinking is lazy. Making America great again can’t mean blind submission. Debate is necessary. Disagreement is good. It reveals how flimsy our assumptions are—if they weren’t, you wouldn’t cry foul over every broken promise or billionaire betrayal.

We—Homo sapiens—are stuck on this rock until we make it better or find a new one. Melanin doesn’t change that. And yes, my shit probably stinks more than yours. So what? Let’s work together anyway. Cooperation is the only survival strategy.

To the MAGA fans and Indian nationalists reading this: the cartoon above isn’t literal. It’s a metaphor—for what goes on inside the white supremacist’s head. You’ll love Vivek Ramaswamy—until you don’t. Because he’s brown. Elon Musk (from South Africa)? Still loved. Rupert Murdoch (from Australia)? Still kingmaker. Why? Because they’re white. Or at least white-adjacent in the American imagination.

Meanwhile, the juicy IT and AI jobs MAGA wants—jobs that aren’t fruit-picking or heavy lifting—will never be theirs. Not because they’re incapable, but because the billionaires don’t want unionizers, job-hoppers, or citizens with rights and healthcare costs. They want quiet workers. Compliant ones. Ones they can offshore if they speak up.

And yes, in the H1B caste system, most of the bodies are South Indian. Not because they’re better—but because of language, community, and comfort filters. The North-South divide is alive and well, even 8,000 miles away. Corruption and mistrust, however, are pan-Indian.

If you’re on an H-1B, you can’t complain. You’ve got ten heads up your ass and no recourse. When I had Jesus Christ’s halo lodged up mine at the Texas VA, I quit. Most Indians won’t. I did. My balls are asymmetrical, small, brown, and hairy. But they’re mine.

Meanwhile, billionaires are holding the country hostage. They always have. You better stock up on Vaseline, MAGA, because those same cocks are going back in. You raised your voice? They’ll outsource the factory. Offshore the cloud. Maybe to some shady island in Asia—*Little Saint James*, minus the minors, plus coders. Same exploitation. Different dress code.

And the Vaseline? Probably imported. But buy it anyway.

You’re gonna need it.

Holy Hallelujah.
